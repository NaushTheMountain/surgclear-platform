1. How SurgClear Operates: End-to-End Operational Workflow

In South Africa's private healthcare system, over 1.2 million elective surgical procedures are performed annually across hospital networks such as Netcare, Life Healthcare, Mediclinic, Busamed, and independent day clinics. Currently, this sector is plagued by severe financial fragmentation, administrative backlogs, and patient billing shock.

The Core Industry Problem: Specialists (surgeons, anaesthetists) routinely bill at 200% to 300% of National Health Reference Price List (RPL) tariffs, whereas medical schemes (Discovery Health, Bonitas, Momentum) only reimburse 100% to 200%. Simultaneously, patients face punitive Designated Service Provider (DSP) network penalties (R8,000 R15,000) if their hospital is out-of-network. Hospital admissions desks still rely on manual clipboards, paper indemnities, and delayed Gap Cover claims.

The 4-Step SurgClear Automated Pipeline

  1. Step 1: Specialist Practice Booking Cockpit (30-Second Trigger)
    When an elective surgery is scheduled, the specialist receptionist or doctor inputs the patient's South African National ID number, selects the surgical procedure template (e.g. RPL 2443 Knee Arthroscopy, ICD-10 M23.2), and adjusts the specialist billing multiplier (100% to 300%). SurgClear verifies scheme membership (Discovery Classic Smart, Bonitas, etc.) and active Gap Cover (Sirago, Turnberry, Zestlife) in real time via electronic switching adapters (MediSwitch / Healthbridge / SpesNet).
  2. Step 2: Real-Time Financial Clearance Engine (Zero-Float ZAR Cent Math)
    The calculation engine immediately computes the comprehensive financial clearance:
    • Hospital Base & Network Status: Determines if the selected hospital is a DSP facility or triggers a deductible (e.g. R12,650.00 non-DSP copayment).
    • Specialist Shortfall Calculation: Calculates exact surgeon and anaesthetist billing shortfalls over the medical scheme base tariff.
    • Gap Cover Offset: Evaluates Gap Cover policies (covering up to 500%600% of tariff shortfall and eligible DSP copayments) against the statutory 2026 Overall Annual Limit (R223,000.00).
    • Net Patient Liability: Calculates the true out-of-pocket balance with an option for 6-Month Medical Financing (e.g. R850.00/month).
  3. Step 3: Magic WhatsApp/SMS Link & Mobile Patient Surgical Pass PWA
    The patient receives a secure link on their smartphone. On the 4-step mobile PWA, the patient:
    • Verifies their medical aid and Gap Cover benefits without phoning call centres.
    • Selects their hospital room type (General Ward vs. Private Suite upgrade +R1,850.00).
    • Chooses specialized dietary pre-admission catering (SANHA-certified Halal, Beth Din Kosher, Diabetic, or Soft Gastric recovery meals).
    • Signs legal POPIA and electronic surgical indemnity consents (compliant with Section 13 of ECTA).
    • Clears upfront co-payments via Stitch Instant EFT or selects 6-Month Medical Financing.
    • Receives their high-contrast, cryptographically verified Digital Boarding Pass QR Code (`ADM-xxxx-JHB`) with an active anaesthetic fasting countdown timer.
  4. Step 4: Automated Pre-Authorisation, Hospital Check-In & Post-Op Gap Claim Filing
    SurgClear generates an authentic HL7 FHIR R4 Institutional Claim JSON payload and switching request, returning an approved pre-authorisation number (`#PA-xxxxxx-ZA`). On admission morning, the hospital receptionist scans the patient's QR code, admitting them in under 45 seconds. Upon discharge, SurgClear automatically packages the specialist invoices and scheme remittance advice, filing the Gap Cover claim directly to Sirago/Turnberry with zero paperwork.
